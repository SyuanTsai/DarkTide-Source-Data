## 執行與計算

普通buff持用時提供melee_weakspot_damage及melee_infinite_cleave_on_headshot。無proc、duration或stacks。[base](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/buff/weapon_traits_buff_templates/base_weapon_trait_buff_templates.lua#L428-L439)。ActionSweep.start重置_num_killed_enemies=0；處理命中時先計質量及執行傷害，再以prior kills<=2、result=died、hit_weakspot、keyword且not ogryn判斷。成立時把本次累計質量回退到命中前、abort_attack=false，並不更新_target_index；之後才將存活角色的擊殺計數加1。因此至多前三次總擊殺可享此分支，並非前三次弱點擊殺，後續目標的順劈傷害序號亦受影響。[條件](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/weapon/actions/action_sweep.lua#L1340-L1389)、[回退](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/weapon/actions/action_sweep.lua#L1416-L1428)、[計數](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/weapon/actions/action_sweep.lua#L1449-L1454)。這不是殺敵後3秒的無限順劈Buff。

非暴擊弱點例子設B為基本傷害、F為未套額外加成的弱點額外部分、s為既有同階段加成、v為本祝福：`D=B+F×(1+s+v)`，之後的共同倍率控制不變。[弱點屬性](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/damage_calculation.lua#L713-L747)、[額外結算](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/damage_calculation.lua#L775-L782)、[加回總傷害](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/damage_calculation.lua#L89-L109)。B=80,F=20,s=0,v=.15時103點；B=50,F=50時107.5點；B=80,F=20,s=.25時105→108點，增幅3÷105≈2.86%。暴擊與弱點同時發生時共用finesse額外部分與其他加成，不能把本例F硬套為獨立的整次傷害倍率。
