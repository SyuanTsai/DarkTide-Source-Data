## Source-confirmed behavior and static derivation

- At the fixed source SHA, the node installs `psyker_ability_increase_brain_burst_speed`. Its proc buff listens to `on_combat_ability`, then adds `psyker_efficient_smites`. That buff lasts 10 seconds and gives `smite_attack_speed=0.75` and `warp_charge_amount_smite=0.5`.
- The locking attack in `psyker_smite.lua` includes `smite_attack_speed` in `time_scale_stat_buffs`. `ActionSmiteTargeting` uses a specified `charge_time` or defaults kill-charge to 3 seconds, then calculates maximum charging time as `charge_time ÷ attack_speed`. Without other speed modifiers, `attack_speed=1+0.75=1.75`, so `2÷1.75≈1.1429` seconds.
- For `psyker_smite` charge templates, `WarpCharge.increase_immediate` multiplies `warp_charge_amount_smite` into the Peril-generation multiplier, giving `10×0.5=5`. Here, “50%” describes halving the Peril generated by that attack.

## Example assumptions and limits

- **How it works**: For 10 seconds after using your Combat Ability, Brain Rupture's charge speed increases by 75% and its generated Peril decreases by 50%.

- **Charging example**: With an original 2-second charge and no other bonuses, 2 ÷ 1.75 ≈ 1.14 seconds. An original 10 Peril percentage points becomes 10 × 0.5 = 5 points.

- Assume a locking charge originally takes 2 seconds and only this talent applies, with other multipliers unchanged. `2 seconds ÷ (1 + 75%) = 2 ÷ 1.75 ≈ 1.14 seconds`. Charge speed becomes 1.75 times the original, shortening charge time by about 42.86%.
- Assume the same move originally generates 10 Peril points and only this talent applies. `10 × 0.5 = 5` Peril points: generated Peril is halved.
- The 2-second and 10-point baselines illustrate formulas. Actual charge time depends on the locking action and other speed modifiers; actual Peril depends on that charge template and existing multipliers.
- The buff's configured duration is 10 seconds. These are static derivations, without in-game measurement; Build 25606770 and the fixed source SHA correspond to 1.13.1. Mechanisms reuse the fixed-version evidence, with local Build 25606770 English text corresponding to 1.13.1. Actual presentation and behavior remain unobserved in game.
