## Source-confirmed trigger

The server-only proc subscribes to `on_hit` with chance `1`. `CheckProcFunctions.on_ranged_weakspot_kills` requires `hit_weakspot`, `attack_result = died`, and either `attack_type = ranged` or a damage profile with `count_as_ranged_attack`. An ordinary melee weakspot kill or a nonlethal weakspot hit does not meet that rule. A specially tagged profile can count as ranged even if its attack-type field is different. [Recovery proc and spawned damage-reduction buff](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/buff/archetype_buff_templates/veteran_buff_templates.lua#L1716-L1750); [Weakspot and combined proc checks](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/buff/helper_functions/check_proc_functions.lua#L473-L520); [CheckProcFunctions.on_ranged_kill](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/buff/helper_functions/check_proc_functions.lua#L216-L224).

`_handle_buffs` supplies the owner event with those fields. Player attacks on allies, the excluded `grimoire` damage type, a profile with `skip_on_hit_proc`, and an already processed `on_hit` event do not emit this normal proc event. The generic proc handler checks the event chance, activation and skill predicate before calling `proc_func`. This template has no configured cooldown, and `_can_activate` permits activation when no cooldown is defined. This is a rule for eligible emitted events, not proof that every special weapon's visual weakspot kill emits one. [Shared attack event fields and exclusions](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/attack.lua#L550-L620); [Excluded damage type](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/attack.lua#L86-L88); [ProcBuff.update_proc_events](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/buff/buffs/proc_buff.lua#L304-L380); [Proc activation and cooldown lookup](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/buff/buffs/proc_buff.lua#L174-L194).

## Recovery and its restrictions

The proc calls `Toughness.replenish_percentage(unit, 0.15, false, "talent_toughness_2")`. The `false` argument allows recovery bonuses. The utility forwards to the owner's toughness extension if present. Define `M` as the extension's current maximum toughness, `R` as `toughness_replenish_modifier`, and `Q` as `toughness_replenish_multiplier`. The nominal recovery is:

```text
Nominal recovery = M × 0.15 × R × Q
```

`max_toughness()` includes the base maximum, extra toughness, its bonus multiplier, rounding and flat bonus. In the normal valid toughness range, actual self-recovery is limited by the deficit; it does not create additional maximum or excess toughness. The code stores damage-to-toughness, subtracts the nominal recovery down to zero, and applies its network representation limit. [Recovery, duration and stack settings](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/talent/talent_settings_veteran.lua#L121-L126); [Recovery proc and spawned damage-reduction buff](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/buff/archetype_buff_templates/veteran_buff_templates.lua#L1716-L1750); [Toughness.replenish_percentage](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/toughness/toughness.lua#L16-L25); [PlayerUnitToughnessExtension.max_toughness](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/toughness/player_unit_toughness_extension.lua#L79-L90); [Percentage recovery and clamps](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/toughness/player_unit_toughness_extension.lua#L253-L285).

Recovery is zero when blocked by being knocked down without forced assistance, being dead, `prevent_toughness_replenish`, or an ability-only recovery restriction whose allowlist excludes `talent_toughness_2`. The proc does not test the returned recovery amount before adding the damage-reduction buff, so being full or blocked from recovering does not itself prevent the stack/refresh attempt when the proc has qualified and the buff extension is present. [Recovery permissions and restrictions](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/toughness/player_unit_toughness_extension.lua#L411-L475); [Recovery proc and spawned damage-reduction buff](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/buff/archetype_buff_templates/veteran_buff_templates.lua#L1716-L1750).

## Stack multiplier and toughness damage

The buff has duration `8`, `max_stacks = 3`, and `toughness_damage_taken_multiplier = 0.9`. Its stat type is `multiplicative_multiplier`. Stat aggregation multiplies the value once for each effective stack; `stat_buff_stacking_count` clamps that count to three. For `n = 0, 1, 2, 3` effective stacks, this talent's factor is `0.9ⁿ`, and its isolated reduction is `1 − 0.9ⁿ`: 0%, 10%, 19% or 27.1%. This is not an additive 30% reduction at three stacks. [Recovery, duration and stack settings](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/talent/talent_settings_veteran.lua#L121-L126); [Recovery proc and spawned damage-reduction buff](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/buff/archetype_buff_templates/veteran_buff_templates.lua#L1716-L1750); [Toughness damage multiplier type](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/buff/buff_settings.lua#L1001-L1004); [Effective stat-stack count](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/buff/buffs/buff.lua#L404-L410); [Stat aggregation per effective stack](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/buff/buffs/buff.lua#L689-L727).

`_calculate_toughness_damage_player` combines the generic factor with melee/ranged toughness factors and additive modifiers before its toughness-damage clamps. Melee attacks then have additional toughness and health-spillover calculations. This talent directly changes toughness damage; its factor must not be presented as the same reduction to all health damage. If no toughness remains, this function bypasses that toughness settlement. [Player toughness damage settlement](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/damage_taken_calculation.lua#L211-L281).

### Controlled damage examples

Assume the incoming attack would deal 100 toughness-damage units at the isolated multiplier stage, enough toughness remains to absorb it, all other modifiers are 1, and no clamp binds. These are arithmetic examples, not measured attacks.

| Effective stacks | Substitution and result | Player meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `1` | `100 × 0.9 = 90` toughness damage; `100 − 90 = 10` prevented. | 10% reduction. |
| `2` | `100 × 0.9² = 81` toughness damage; `100 − 81 = 19` prevented. | 19% reduction. |
| `3` | `100 × 0.9³ = 72.9` toughness damage; `100 − 72.9 = 27.1` prevented. | 27.1% reduction. |

With the same assumptions and an independent toughness multiplier of `0.80`, three stacks give `100 × 0.80 × 0.9³ = 58.32` toughness-damage units. Total reduction relative to the unmodified 100 is `1 − 0.80 × 0.9³ = 41.68%`; the reductions are not added as `20% + 27.1%`. Relative to the already reduced 80, this talent still removes `80 × 0.271 = 21.68` units, or 27.1% of that 80. This example applies only to multiplicative factors at the traced stage. [Stat aggregation per effective stack](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/buff/buffs/buff.lua#L689-L727); [Player toughness damage settlement](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/damage_taken_calculation.lua#L211-L281).

## Shared timer, decay and internal overflow

Both `refresh_duration_on_stack` and `refresh_duration_on_remove_stack` are true. Adding a stack to the shared buff object resets its start time. Expiry uses the strict test `t > start_time + 8`, not equality. When a removal begins with two or three stacks, one stack is removed and the common start time is reset; that reset clears the expired flag. The last stack's expiry deletes the buff. Thus the effective stacks decay one at a time, with a new interval after each reduction. [Recovery proc and spawned damage-reduction buff](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/buff/archetype_buff_templates/veteran_buff_templates.lua#L1716-L1750); [Shared buff instance and timer refresh](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/buff/buff_extension_base.lua#L434-L488); [Strict expiry test](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/buff/buffs/buff.lua#L29-L44); [Stack removal, timer reset and index removal](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/buff/buff_extension_base.lua#L635-L698); [Start-time reset clears expiry](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/buff/buffs/buff.lua#L619-L639).

Assume exactly three internal stacks after the last qualifying kill at time `t₀`, no new triggers or forced removals, and ordinary updates. At the first update after `t₀ + 8 seconds`, three becomes two. Roughly 8 more seconds later, two becomes one; roughly another 8 seconds later, the effect ends. The approximate 8/16/24-second timeline is static branch derivation; actual update boundaries add delay. Another eligible kill resets the shared countdown even at the effective cap.

`max_stacks = 3` is the effective-stat cap, not a hard cap on the internal counter. This template has no `max_stacks_cap`; `Buff.add_stack` increases the counter without clamping it, while multiple indexes refer to the same object. On expiry, index iteration can remove overflow counts in the same update without timer refresh while the pre-removal count exceeds three. The three-to-two removal then resets the timer. Extra internal counts therefore do not add damage reduction, and must not be promised an extra 8 seconds each. Exact same-frame ordering and runtime behavior of those counters have not been measured. [Hard-cap check](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/buff/buff_extension_base.lua#L560-L589); [Internal stack changes](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/buff/buffs/buff.lua#L453-L515); [Shared buff instance and timer refresh](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/buff/buff_extension_base.lua#L434-L488); [Expiry iteration over buff indexes](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/buff/buff_extension_base.lua#L187-L206); [Stack removal, timer reset and index removal](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/buff/buff_extension_base.lua#L635-L698); [Effective stat-stack count](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/buff/buffs/buff.lua#L404-L410).

## Related recovery event

The replenishment event carries both nominal `amount` and actual `recovered_amount`. The related `veteran_share_toughness_gained` proc reads nominal `amount`, ignores events with reason `shared`, and restores toughness to other units in coherency. Therefore full toughness on this talent's owner does not establish that the sharing amount is zero: a permitted positive nominal grant can emit an event even when actual self-recovery is zero. Recovery blocked before event emission is a different case. This confirms the event interaction; the sharing talent's full values, range and player description require their own evidence. [Percentage recovery and clamps](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/toughness/player_unit_toughness_extension.lua#L253-L285); [Replenishment event amounts](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/toughness/player_unit_toughness_extension.lua#L477-L491); [Sharing proc reads nominal amount](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/buff/archetype_buff_templates/veteran_buff_templates.lua#L2330-L2377).
