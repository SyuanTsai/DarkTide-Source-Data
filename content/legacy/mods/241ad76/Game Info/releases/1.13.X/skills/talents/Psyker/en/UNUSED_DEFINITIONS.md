## Interpretation limits

- Five listed effects or special rules remain provided by current selectable talents: Gaze's Toughness protection, Quietude's restoration on Peril generation, Warp Siphon's Damage and cooldown effects, and Smite's chain adjustment. They are included in those talents and are not classified as ineffective.
- The other eleven distinguish retained implementations that are not installed, rules that are not read, and residual definitions without a template; they are not all labelled disabled.
- Not-installed findings cover this fixed version's archetype base list, 81 selectable nodes and their actual buff/special-rule references. They do not claim that other modes, test tools or future versions cannot reference the definitions.
- Local Steam Build 25606770 corresponds to public source version 1.13.1. No in-game testing was performed.
