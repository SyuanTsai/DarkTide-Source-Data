## Stance, handling and cooldown

- Activation equips the secondary/ranged slot and grants the **six-second** stance. The selected base talent does not install the automatic-reload special rule checked by the action. [Selected stance ability](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/ability/player_abilities/abilities/veteran_abilities.lua#L65-L72); [Activate stance and equip ranged slot](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/ability/actions/action_veteran_combat_ability.lua#L91-L112); [Base talent and display definitions](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/ability/archetype_talents/talents/veteran_talents.lua#L206-L247); [Conditional automatic-reload rule](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/ability/actions/action_veteran_combat_ability.lua#L180-L198); [Stance handling and immunity keywords](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/buff/archetype_buff_templates/veteran_buff_templates.lua#L85-L109).
- The stance master applies spread `−0.38`, recoil `−0.24` and sway multiplier `0.4`: 38% less spread, 24% less recoil and 60% less sway. It carries `stun_immune`, `slowdown_immune`, `suppression_immune`, `ranged_alternate_fire_interrupt_immune`, `uninterruptible`, `deterministic_recoil` and the stance keyword. These keywords are not a guarantee against every incapacitating attack. [Stance handling and immunity keywords](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/buff/archetype_buff_templates/veteran_buff_templates.lua#L85-L109); [Base and display damage settings](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/talent/talent_settings_veteran.lua#L73-L100).
- Its conditional exit checks the disabled state, rather than weapon switching. Switching to melee does not by itself end the stance; being Knocked Down or otherwise disabled does. [Disabled-state exit](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/buff/archetype_buff_templates/veteran_buff_templates.lua#L202-L207).
- The unmodified ability costs **30 resource units** and naturally regenerates **1 unit/s**. Consumption begins on activation, and the stance configuration does not pause recharge. The **30-second** baseline cooldown therefore overlaps the six-second stance. [Base and display damage settings](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/talent/talent_settings_veteran.lua#L73-L100); [Base resource settings](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/ability/player_abilities/abilities/veteran_abilities.lua#L7-L24); [Selected stance ability](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/ability/player_abilities/abilities/veteran_abilities.lua#L65-L72); [Ability activation/resource consumption](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/ability/player_unit_ability_extension.lua#L826-L869); [Action ability start](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/weapon/actions/action_ability_base.lua#L25-L43); [Shared ability action configuration](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/ability/ability_templates/veteran_combat_ability.lua#L5-L100).

## Different damage stages

- `ranged_damage = 0.15` adds at the general damage-multiplier stage. `ranged_weakspot_damage = 0.15` instead modifies the extra weakspot/finesse portion. General damage is settled before finesse is calculated and added back. [General additive damage stage](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/damage_calculation.lua#L284-L320); [Damage calculation order](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/damage_calculation.lua#L60-L95); [Extra finesse calculation](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/damage_calculation.lua#L672-L782).
- For a weakspot-only comparison, hold the upstream base component `B` and unbonused extra component `F` fixed: `D_before = B + F`; `D_after = B + F × 1.15`, assuming no critical hit or other finesse bonus. This isolates the weakspot modifier; it is not the complete before/after result of activating Volley Fire. [Installed base damage/impact buff](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/buff/archetype_buff_templates/veteran_buff_templates.lua#L296-L315); [Extra finesse calculation](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/damage_calculation.lua#L672-L782).
- A full ability comparison must recalculate the general ranged modifier's effect on both `B` and `F`, including finesse's maximum and lower-bound branches. Neither **+30% to the whole weakspot hit** nor a universal **×1.15** represents the combined ability. The impact stat is also distinct from damage and from actual stagger duration. [Base and outline-upgrade stat definitions](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/buff/archetype_buff_templates/veteran_buff_templates.lua#L296-L323); [Damage calculation order](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/damage_calculation.lua#L60-L95); [Extra finesse calculation](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/damage_calculation.lua#L672-L782).

## Isolated damage, handling and cooldown examples

Assume the stated input at the affected stage, no other bonuses except the explicitly listed 25% general bonus, and no downstream modifiers. Weakspot rows hold the already settled upstream `B` and `F` fixed, exclude critical hits and other finesse bonuses, and compare only the extra weakspot modifier. Handling rows use an isolated normalized input of 1. Timing ignores update-frame offsets. These are static examples, not weapon or game measurements.

| Assumptions | Substitution and result | Player meaning |
|---|---|---|
| General ranged-damage stage input 100; no other same-stage bonus | `100 × (1 + 0.15) = 115 damage units`. | This isolates the general ranged bonus, before extra finesse and later stages. |
| General stage input 100; existing additive 25% bonus | Before `100 × 1.25 = 125`; after `100 × (1 + 0.25 + 0.15) = 140`; `(140 − 125) / 125 = 12%`. | Adding 15 percentage points at this stage gives 12% more than the already-bonused stage result. |
| Weakspot modifier only; `B = 100`, `F = 40` | `100 + 40 = 140` becomes `100 + 40 × 1.15 = 146 damage units`; `6 / 140 ≈ 4.29%`. | The 15% extra-component bonus gives about 4.29% more for this isolated hit, not 15% to the whole hit. |
| Weakspot modifier only; `B = 100`, `F = 100` | `100 + 100 = 200` becomes `100 + 100 × 1.15 = 215 damage units`; `15 / 200 = 7.5%`. | A larger extra component changes the whole-hit gain; this is still not the ability's combined gain. |
| Isolated handling input 1 for each property | Spread `1 × (1 − 0.38) = 0.62`; recoil `1 × (1 − 0.24) = 0.76`; sway `1 × 0.4 = 0.4`. | These correspond to 38%, 24% and 60% reductions in their respective configured factors. |
| Unmodified use; stance lasts 6s and base recharge takes 30s | Resource recharge `30 units / (1 unit/s) = 30s`; at stance end, `30 − 6 = 24 seconds` remain. | Recharge runs during the stance, so there is about a 24-second wait after its ordinary end. |

## Original English template and reconstruction

Full raw template, hash `a34966f2`:

```text
Enter Ranged Stance for {duration:%s}s. When in Ranged Stance you instantly equip your ranged weapon, deal {damage:%s} Ranged Damage & {weakspot_damage:%s} Ranged Weakspot Damage. Your Spread & Recoil are also greatly reduced.

Base Cooldown: {cooldown:%s}s.
```

| Parameter | Definition and formatting | Reconstructed value |
|---|---|---|
| `damage` | Display `combat_ability.ranged_damage = 0.25`; signed percentage formatting | `+25%`; installed base effect is 15% |
| `weakspot_damage` | Display `combat_ability.ranged_weakspot_damage = 0.25`; signed percentage formatting | `+25%`; installed base effect is 15% |
| `duration` | `combat_ability.duration = 6`; number formatting | `6` |
| `cooldown` | Selected ability's base cooldown is 30; number formatting | `30` |

Static plain-text reconstruction, preserving both original paragraphs:

```text
Enter Ranged Stance for 6s. When in Ranged Stance you instantly equip your ranged weapon, deal +25% Ranged Damage & +25% Ranged Weakspot Damage. Your Spread & Recoil are also greatly reduced.

Base Cooldown: 30s.
```

This excludes runtime color markup and is not an observed game screen. The reconstructed +25% ranged and +25% ranged-weakspot values contradict the installed **base** buff's +15%/+15% values. Duration, equip behavior, generic spread/recoil reduction and base cooldown agree. The extra-component scope, impact, exact handling/immunity, recharge start, disabled exit and absence of automatic reload supplement the short description.

## Limits

These results describe the base start-node ability, before upgrades add further effects. Exact timing, handling, weapon damage, incapacitation boundaries and final game UI have not been measured. The isolated damage rows must not be added together or presented as a complete weapon comparison.
