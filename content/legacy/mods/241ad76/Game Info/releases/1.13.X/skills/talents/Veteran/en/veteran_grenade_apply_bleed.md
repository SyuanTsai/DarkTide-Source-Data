## Bleed timing and damage

- Bleed has an approximately **0.5-second damage interval**, **1.5-second refreshed duration** and **16-stack maximum**. Reapplication refreshes the duration. With `interval_stack_removal`, expiry leads to stack removal on successive interval updates rather than all stacks vanishing at once after 1.5s. Exact update ordering remains a timing limit. [Bleed interval, duration and stack settings](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/buff/weapon_buff_templates.lua#L235-L260); [IntervalBuff tick and stack removal](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/buff/buffs/interval_buff.lua#L17-L64).
- For `n` effective stacks, set `x = n / 16` and `smoothstep(x) = x² × (3 − 2x)`. The bleed power input is `500 × smoothstep(n / 16)`; the damage distribution is `175`. With output-damage scale `20`, normalization maximum `10,000` and Unarmoured armor multiplier `0.5`, the isolated per-tick result is `175 × smoothstep(n / 16) × 0.5`. [Bleed stack power curve](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/damage/damage_profiles/buff_damage_profile_templates.lua#L59-L68); [Separate bleed damage profile](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/damage/damage_profiles/buff_damage_profile_templates.lua#L443-L466); [Default power input](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/power_level.lua#L21-L23); [Power modifier application](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/power_level.lua#L63-L94); [Power-level normalization values](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/damage/power_level_settings.lua#L7-L29); [Power-scaled damage distribution](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/damage_profile.lua#L377-L444).
- This is a nonlinear stack curve, not a flat damage amount per stack or a universal DPS value. Armor, hit zone, power, damage, Rending and other modifiers can change the result. [Separate bleed damage profile](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/damage/damage_profiles/buff_damage_profile_templates.lua#L443-L466); [Power modifier application](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/power_level.lua#L63-L94); [Power-scaled damage distribution](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/damage_profile.lua#L377-L444).
- The Frag explosion and bleed are separate damage components. [Grenade Tinkerer](veteran_improved_grenades.md) increases the explosion damage/radius by 25%, but its Frag explosion damage stat is not applied to the separate bleed profile. [Frag damage profile and armor multipliers](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/damage/damage_profiles/demolitions_damage_profile_templates.lua#L213-L258); [Separate bleed damage profile](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/damage/damage_profiles/buff_damage_profile_templates.lua#L443-L466); [Grenade Tinkerer modifiers](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/buff/archetype_buff_templates/veteran_buff_templates.lua#L1297-L1306); [Profile-specific damage-stat application](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/damage_calculation.lua#L570-L584).

## English comparison and limits

The original describes the damaging explosion, six-stack damage-over-time effect and augmentation of Frag Grenade. The survival/damaging-hit conditions, stack cap, refresh/decay and calculation details supplement that explosion description. No English erratum is supported.

Actual explosion and bleed damage, repeated-grant/update timing, target eligibility and final game UI have not been measured in game. The per-tick examples do not imply that all stacks stay at the same count through expiry.
