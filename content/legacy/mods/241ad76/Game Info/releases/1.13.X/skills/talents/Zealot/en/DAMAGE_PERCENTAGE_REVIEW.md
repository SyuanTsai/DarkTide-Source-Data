# Zealot: percentages and example review

[繁體中文](../DAMAGE_PERCENTAGE_REVIEW.md) | [Player descriptions](README.md) | [Technical index](SOURCE_INDEX.md)

This inventory covers 82 selectable nodes. Each row retains recipients, actual examples and limits: Weakspot/Critical extra damage is not the whole-hit increase, rate is not a time reduction, and fixed recovery points are not a percentage of maximum. Results are static derivations, not in-game tests.
