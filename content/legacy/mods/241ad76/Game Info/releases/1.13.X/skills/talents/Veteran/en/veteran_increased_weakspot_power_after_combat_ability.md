## Trigger and timing

- Non-Stealth combat abilities grant the buff through `veteran_buffs_after_combat_ability`. [Infiltrate](veteran_invisibility_on_combat_ability.md) grants it at Stealth start. The template has `duration = 10`, `max_stacks = max_stacks_cap = 1`, and `weakspot_power_level_modifier = 0.2`. [Shared combat ability trigger](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/buff/archetype_buff_templates/veteran_buff_templates.lua#L1977-L2014); [Grant on Stealth start](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/buff/archetype_buff_templates/veteran_buff_templates.lua#L1227-L1229); [Weakspot power buff](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/buff/archetype_buff_templates/veteran_buff_templates.lua#L2048-L2063).
- The custom buff is active during Stealth. It records `_stop_t = t + 10` only when it first observes Stealth absent, so coverage continues for ten seconds after leaving Stealth. With a non-Stealth ability, this countdown begins at the first absence-check update. [Custom exit countdown](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/buff/buffs/veteran_stealth_bonuses_buff.lua#L15-L44); [Weakspot power buff](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/buff/archetype_buff_templates/veteran_buff_templates.lua#L2048-L2063).
- Reapplication does not stack the effect. Shared refresh changes `_start_time`, but does not reset the custom `_stop_t` or `_in_invisibility`; an already-running ten-second countdown is not restarted. [Weakspot power buff](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/buff/archetype_buff_templates/veteran_buff_templates.lua#L2048-L2063); [Shared stack refresh](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/buff/buffs/buff.lua#L619-L639); [Start-time setter](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/buff/buffs/buff.lua#L339-L348); [Custom exit countdown](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/buff/buffs/veteran_stealth_bonuses_buff.lua#L15-L44).

## Weakspot power, damage and impact

- `weakspot_power_level_modifier` is an `additive_multiplier`. `PowerLevel.power_level_buff_modifier` includes it only when `weakspot_or_nil` is true, and adds it to other power bonuses at that stage. Both melee and ranged weakspot hits can benefit. [Stat types and neutral values](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/buff/buff_settings.lua#L1030-L1057); [Buff stat aggregation](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/buff/buffs/buff.lua#L689-L727); [PowerLevel calculation and weakspot gate](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/power_level.lua#L25-L91); [Weakspot power supplied to damage](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/damage_calculation.lua#L219-L228).
- This is a **PowerLevel-stage** bonus, unlike [Precision Strikes](veteran_increased_weakspot_damage.md)'s `weakspot_damage` increase to the extra finesse component. Do not reuse its `0.30F / (B + F)` formula for Marksman. Weapon damage-profile minimum/maximum outputs, finesse floors, armor and hit-location modifiers still affect the complete hit. [PowerLevel entry points](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/power_level.lua#L21-L23); [PowerLevel calculation and weakspot gate](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/power_level.lua#L25-L91); [Weakspot power supplied to damage](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/damage_calculation.lua#L219-L228); [Damage-profile output ranges](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/damage_profile.lua#L386-L445); [Later finesse calculation](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/damage_calculation.lua#L672-L710).
- For the default curve example, `ratio = native_ratio = 10`: input power 500 remains 500 after the curve, then the isolated weakspot modifier yields `500 × 1.2 = 600`. [PowerLevel calculation and weakspot gate](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/power_level.lua#L25-L91); [Default curve ratios](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/damage/power_level_settings.lua#L55-L62).
- The default damage example uses attack distribution 100, unarmored armor-damage modifier 1, output range `0..20`, `max_power_level = 10000`, hit-zone multiplier 1, and excludes extra finesse and other damage multipliers. This gives `20 × (power × 100) / 10000`. It is a reproducible synthetic baseline, not a named weapon's measured final damage. [Default attack distributions](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/damage_profile.lua#L29-L34); [Default armor output settings](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/damage/power_level_settings.lua#L89-L122); [Maximum power settings](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/damage/power_level_settings.lua#L7-L25); [Damage-profile output ranges](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/damage_profile.lua#L386-L445); [Base damage calculation](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/damage_calculation.lua#L587-L615).
- `StaggerCalculation` passes `hit_weakspot` into impact power. With default impact distribution 5, unarmored stagger output `0..20` and other factors 1, the example is `20 × (power × 5) / 10000`. Later enemy resistance, thresholds, stagger buffs/count and state checks still determine the outcome. A larger impact input does not guarantee 20% longer stagger. [Weakspot power supplied to impact](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/stagger_calculation.lua#L67-L92); [Default attack distributions](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/damage_profile.lua#L29-L34); [Default armor output settings](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/damage/power_level_settings.lua#L89-L122); [Maximum power settings](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/settings/damage/power_level_settings.lua#L7-L25).
- `DamageProfile.max_hit_mass` supplies `weakspot = nil` for cleave power, so this modifier does not increase the cleave budget. It does not grant extra cleave or penetration targets by this route. [Cleave power omits the weakspot flag](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/damage_profile.lua#L36-L48); [PowerLevel calculation and weakspot gate](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/power_level.lua#L25-L91).

## Power, damage, impact and timing examples

Use the synthetic default settings above, a weakspot hit and one active instance. Assume no other power modifiers unless listed; exclude extra finesse and downstream damage/impact modifiers. Timer examples ignore update-frame boundaries and assume the same active instance is reapplied. These are static calculations, not weapon or game measurements.

| Assumptions | Substitution and result | Player meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Input power 500; no other power bonuses | `500 × (1 + 0.20) = 600 power units`. | Marksman increases the weakspot attack's power input. |
| Default base-damage example | Before: `20 × (500 × 100) / 10000 = 100`; after: `20 × (600 × 100) / 10000 = 120 damage units`. | The isolated base stage gains 20%; the complete weakspot hit still requires later calculations. |
| Existing same-stage power bonus 25% | Before: `100 × 1.25 = 125`; after: `100 × (1 + 0.25 + 0.20) = 145`; gain `(145 − 125) / 125 = 16%`. | An added 20 percentage points of power give 16% more base damage relative to this already-bonused baseline. |
| Default impact example | Before: `20 × (500 × 5) / 10000 = 5`; after: `20 × (600 × 5) / 10000 = 6 impact units`. | Impact input increases; enemy thresholds and resistance still decide stagger behavior and duration. |
| Leave Infiltrate at about 3s after activation | Coverage lasts to about `3 + 10 = 13s` after activation. | The bonus is already active during Stealth, followed by the exit countdown. |
| First countdown starts at 0s; same instance is reapplied at about 4s | Deadline remains near `10s`, leaving `10 − 4 = 6s`, rather than moving to `14s`. | Reapplying during this countdown does not restart it. |

[PowerLevel calculation and weakspot gate](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/power_level.lua#L25-L91); [Base damage calculation](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/damage_calculation.lua#L587-L615); [Weakspot power supplied to impact](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/utilities/attack/stagger_calculation.lua#L67-L92); [Custom exit countdown](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/buff/buffs/veteran_stealth_bonuses_buff.lua#L15-L44); [Shared stack refresh](https://github.com/Aussiemon/Darktide-Source-Code/blob/7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e/scripts/extension_systems/buff/buffs/buff.lua#L619-L639).

## Original English template and reconstruction

Full raw template, hash `94eac308`:

```text
On Ability use, for {duration%s}s any Weakspot hits gain {power%s} Strength.

When using {talent_name:%s}, this is applied after leaving Stealth.
```

The talent places `value_manipulation = value − 1` inside its `find_value` table. The shown lookup returns the raw path value `0.2`; parameter formatting then passes the **outer** config to the percentage formatter. The nested manipulation is not applied in this path, so the reconstruction is `+20%`, not `-80%`. The raw `{duration%s}` and `{power%s}` tokens intentionally retain their missing colons, which the interpolation pattern supports. `Strength` matches the weakspot power effect; it does not promise a uniform 20% increase to every weapon's final damage. Additive composition, impact/cleave distinctions and countdown refresh rules are omitted details.

## Limits

The 100→120 and 5→6 examples depend on the stated default distributions/output ranges and deliberately simplified multipliers. They are not measured weapon damage or stagger duration. Actual weapon profiles, finesse/armor/hit-zone stages, target resistance and thresholds must still be applied. Exact timer frames and reapplication measured in game remain unobserved.
